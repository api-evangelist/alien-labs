# Alien Labs

> Alien Labs, Inc. was a San Francisco software startup (founders Cristian Strat, Mircea Pasoi, Liana Dumitru; seed investor Bloomberg Beta) behind Summify — a social news digest acquired by Twitter in January 2012 — and Memo, a Slack-native team wiki used by 8,000+ teams. Memo closed signups on 2018-01-16 and shut down on 2018-03-16, with most of the engineering team joining Coinbase. The company is defunct and publishes no API, SDK, or developer documentation.

## Products (historical)

- [Memo](https://web.archive.org/web/20180401/https://memo.ai/): Slack-native team knowledge base — star a Slack message to save it as a note, rich-text/code-block editor, boards, search, auto-reminders, `/memo-find` slash command. Shut down 2018-03-16.
- [Summify](https://techcrunch.com/2012/01/19/twitter-acquires-summify/): Personalized social news digest over Twitter and Facebook. Acquired by Twitter 2012-01-19 and wound down.
